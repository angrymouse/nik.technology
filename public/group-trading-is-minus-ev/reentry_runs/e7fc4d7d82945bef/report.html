Repeated calls and bounded re-entry

Retrospective research on the existing dates with 406 additional recoverable-text repeated calls. Later-edited messages are excluded because their original contents cannot be recovered; this is a data-availability restriction that can bias the sample, not a deployable future-edit filter. Existing fixed candidates and validation windows are unchanged.

Canonicalize entry masks only on completed earlier training calls. Two clipped-return reference screens: first executable call per contract; all qualifying calls weighted equally within each contract and contracts weighted equally. Both include a $20-or-volume-limit position, fees and a 10% sale haircut. Require twelve contracts; rank the larger mean-minus-dispersion score. Keep sixteen entry/exit/delay candidates per group, at most two per training mask. This heuristic shortlist is not exhaustive portfolio optimization or a confidence bound.

Ordinary selectors require ten entered trades/eight contracts, positive return at both 5% and 10% costs and the training drawdown ceiling; maximize 10%-cost return. Robust selectors require fourteen trades/ten contracts/three trading days and positive 10%-cost return after excluding two best contracts by aggregate PnL and after excluding the best entry day. Cash is allowed. The last fit uses the full old dataset and adds no evaluation day.
