Dependence on evaluation days

Post-hoc day dependence, not cross-validation or confidence intervals. Saved daily rules and observed group history are unchanged. Each replay prevents all entries on the excluded UTC day and recomputes later cash, overlap, contract eligibility, budgets and risk controls. Multiple procedures share the same tokens and dates; they are not independent trials.
