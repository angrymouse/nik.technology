Activity filters and sizing

Retrospective daily refits on examined dates. Earlier saved entry/cohort gates and daily full-loss budgets remain fixed; activity multipliers, allocation scale and exit are selected on earlier completed outcomes.

207 activity policies: short/long volume rates, changing buy share, within-transaction net flow, active seconds, gaps since the last trade, recipient concentration, recipient breadth, 15-second returns and estimated fee burden. Policies reject a failing condition or reduce its size to 25% or 50%. Four allocation multipliers and six exits per day are compared. The maximum hold is one, three, five or ten minutes; ten-minute rules can take partial profits.

14,904 training policies considered; 13,476 distinct portfolio evaluations after reusing equivalent stake vectors on completed training calls. Model account $1,000, 1% preceding-volume participation and historical gas/MEV estimates.
