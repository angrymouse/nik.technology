Caller dependence

Post-hoc dependence diagnostics, not a learned caller blacklist. Exclude entries from one Telegram sender, preserve all observed history/features, then replay cash, overlap, budgets and later trades. Caller figures describe calls under these rules, not personal trading returns or wallet ownership.

Ranking uses observed contribution at the 5% cost setting. Each exclusion recomputes the entire chronological portfolio. It is not a subtraction from the original ledger.

Excluding a sender can replace their entry with another sender's call on the same token. Replacement counts are included in the per-caller table; unchanged returns therefore do not prove independence from the winning tokens.

Most caller samples contain very few trades. These rankings are descriptions of this replay and must not be treated as evidence that a caller will be profitable or unprofitable next time.
