Recent group performance and trade size

Scope: 766 human-authored eligible calls across three replay days, with 520 distinct tokens first called by a human. A group score uses the first eligible human call per other token, an observed 60- or 180-second sale, and at least five completed reference outcomes. It is frozen at the target call's first quote; unfinished outcomes and the target token are excluded. Scores use the last 10 or 20 completed tokens, or the preceding one or six hours. The replay varies the group score's sale haircut, size mapping, and trade sale haircut. There are 144 score rules, including 96 reversal rules. These dates were already examined, so the best variant is post hoc.

The post hoc best variant has higher account return than the inherited size rule in 100 of 100 scenarios, but higher profit per dollar staked in only 49. Its median total stake is 6.83 against 5.18. Its profit advantage does not establish a better group signal.

The best reversal rule in this grid has median total stake 7.88, close to 8.11 for a flat 1× size. Against that flat rule it has higher account return in 22 of 100 matched execution cases and higher profit per dollar staked in 26.
