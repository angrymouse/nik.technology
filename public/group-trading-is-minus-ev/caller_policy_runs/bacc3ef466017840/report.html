Same saved daily entries, budgets, six exits and four scales. Require positive 5%/10%-cost training returns and the named training drawdown ceiling; maximize the 10%-cost return. Each identity family may select unmodified sizing or cash. Ties retain catalog order.

History can use actual senders or explicitly identified scanner requesters. It covers 60/180-second reference outcomes, all preceding history or 24 hours, all tokens or the same chain/cap band. Shrinkage uses peer outcomes from earlier unique tokens. Poor scores reject a call or halve its allocation. Unknown callers can be allowed or excluded; both are explicit catalog choices.
