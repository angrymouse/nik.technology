What was measured

Sell 25%, 50% or 75% of the original quantity at completed-candle targets from 1.1x through 3x, or use two staged targets. Crossed targets combine into one order. Orders are serialized and fill at the next observed open after the reaction delay. Manage the remainder with the original stop/trail, an entry-price floor, or a gross-principal recovery floor that does not include fees. Reserve the maximum possible transaction fees before entry; charge an extra prior-median gas/MEV fee for each partial attempt. Drawdown uses realized cash plus remaining inventory. An unfilled fraction is written off after ten seconds. A partial timeout also marks the remaining position at zero at that instant for a conservative missing-price bound.

Default features stop five seconds before the call. One- and fifteen-second lags are saved as sensitivity replays. Routes net target-token quantities per transaction and recipient. Fees count once per transaction. No wallet is attributed to a Telegram member. The volume window does not establish historical pool reserves or AMM impact.
