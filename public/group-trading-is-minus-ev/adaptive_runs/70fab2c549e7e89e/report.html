Execution assumptions

  • The model is Perplexity Decider v1.2-20261016. The bot's v1.1 returned HTTP 404, no endpoints found.
  • Every strategy enters at the next observed minute open after one additional full minute for decision/execution. With a 2-minute feature wait, entry is at minute 3.
  • Requests contain only pre-entry features. Model requests slower than the available wait are not tradable for model-dependent rules.
  • The model has no token addresses, usernames, links, ticker identifiers, future bars, or future return labels in its supplied state. Narrative text can retain recognizable clues.
  • Quotes and cap filters are historical. Confirmed cap reprices the historical cap with the latest completed candle, assuming unchanged supply.
  • Caller, cap-bucket, and model-score history freezes at UTC midnight and uses only earlier completed 3-minute paper trades.
  • One unleveraged open position. Stake is a fraction of current equity, capped at 7.5%. No historical market-impact model is available.
  • Daily loss budgets reserve the full position loss, not the distance to a stop. Closed-position risk state and seen contracts carry between evaluation days.
  • Stops win ambiguous target/stop candles, gap-down stops use the worse open, stale remaining holdings are written off, and sold partial proceeds survive.
  • All daily selectors and all-data fits are retrospective. The screening penalty and calibration tables are descriptive, not selection-adjusted statistical evidence.
  • Cap and caller tables are overlapping 3-minute paper trades with a 5% proceeds haircut; they are not portfolio returns or permanent blacklists.
  • Only minute candles are available. Sub-minute holds and realized on-chain execution quality cannot be established from this dataset.
