404 exit choices per stage, 9 sizing policies and 21,816 portfolio evaluations. Training uses only outcomes completed before that day's cutoff. Selectors require positive 5% and 10% cost results, at least ten trades and eight tokens, and their named training drawdown limit. The robust selector additionally requires fourteen trades, ten tokens and a positive 10%-cost result after removing the two best training tokens.

Primary account: $1,000. Positions cannot exceed 1% of preceding 60-second economic volume. Base round-trip gas and MEV are twice the preceding median, plus the proceeds haircut. These are proxy assumptions.

Each partial attempt adds one prior-median gas/MEV fee. Maximum possible fees are reserved before entry; actual fees depend on submitted orders. Sales fill after the signal and reaction delay, not at their target price. The entry-price and gross-principal floors do not cover fees. Drawdown tracks realized proceeds and remaining inventory. Unfilled fractions are written off after ten seconds; a partial timeout also marks the remaining position at zero at that instant for a conservative missing-price bound.
