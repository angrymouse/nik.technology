Conditional entries and activity sizing

1,705,600 entry/exit/filter combinations on the existing historical dataset. Entry families: dip, recovery after a dip, breakout of the initial range, momentum and a fixed-wait control. Entries expire after 30–180 seconds. Price-collapse cancellation and a 5% maximum chase are compared. Market cap uses either the original quote or the price observed when the trigger occurs.

The 5% and 2% selectors constrain training drawdown, not future losses. The robust selector additionally requires a profitable training replay after removing its most profitable token. The minimum training sample is ten entered trades and eight contracts.

Execution and sizing

Every trigger uses completed second candles only. A market entry follows one second later, at the next observed trade within ten seconds. Stops and targets also incur a one-second reaction delay. No entry fill means no purchase; a missing exit fill is written down to zero. Fractions of 1%, 2% and 5% are compared with flat, activity, recent range, relative-volume and combined sizing. Only prior activity and relative volume are used; no dollar capacity is inferred from vendor candle volume.

Portfolios hold one position, trade each contract once, cap allocations at 7.5% of equity and use a full-loss daily budget. Costs are modeled haircuts to sale proceeds; actual AMM impact, MEV and transaction failures are not measured.
