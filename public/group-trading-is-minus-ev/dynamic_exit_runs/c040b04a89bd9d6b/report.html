What was measured

Time-dependent stops, stalled-price exits, volatility-adjusted trailing stops, volume fade and short-term price reversals. Maximum holds range from five seconds to ten minutes. All trigger decisions use completed one-second candles; exits fill at the next observed open after the reaction delay, with a ten-second wait limit. Training combines fine cap, caller, cohort and chain filters with flow-based sizing. Pre-call lag sensitivity affects volume, fees and sizing; it does not change the quote-time metadata filters.

Default features stop five seconds before the call. One- and fifteen-second lags are saved as sensitivity replays. Routes net target-token quantities per transaction and recipient. Fees count once per transaction. No wallet is attributed to a Telegram member. The volume window does not establish historical pool reserves or AMM impact.
