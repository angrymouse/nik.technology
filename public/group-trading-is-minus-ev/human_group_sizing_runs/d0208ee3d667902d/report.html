Recent group performance and trade size

Human-authored calls only. A group score uses the first eligible human call per other token, an observed 60- or 180-second sale, and at least five completed reference outcomes. It is frozen at the target call's first quote; unfinished outcomes and the target token are excluded. Scores use the last 10 or 20 completed tokens, or the preceding one or six hours. The replay varies the group score's sale haircut, size mapping, and trade sale haircut. These dates were already examined, so the best variant is post hoc.
