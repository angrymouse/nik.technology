What was measured

Recipient addresses with at least $5 net buying in the preceding 180 seconds. The context uses their earlier associations with first calls on other tokens, followed by fixed 60- or 180-second paper outcomes after a 10% proceeds haircut. Only fully completed outcomes are available. Histories use the past day or all prior data, at most 20 tokens per address, and three zero-return observations for shrinkage. Sizing uses history scores or scores relative to the prior token cohort. These are predictive contexts, not wallet PnL, ownership claims or Telegram identity links. Catalog seed: 20261023.

Default features stop five seconds before the call. One- and fifteen-second lags are saved as sensitivity replays. Routes net target-token quantities per transaction and recipient. Fees count once per transaction. No wallet is attributed to a Telegram member. The volume window does not establish historical pool reserves or AMM impact.
