Account risk controls

Retrospective daily selections on repeatedly examined dates. Training drawdown constraints are not live loss guarantees. Controls do not change frozen validation protocols.

Controls see only realized cash, prior closed-equity high and completed entered-trade results. Loss streaks, recovery factors, daily peaks and pauses reset on the next UTC entry day; overall closed-equity high carries across days. A pause or rejection leaves later calls on an untraded token eligible. Original one-position, fee-reserve and volume limits remain in force.

Same saved daily entry/cohort rules, budgets and six exits per day. Require positive training returns at 5%/10% sale costs and the named training drawdown ceiling; maximize the 10%-cost return. The robust selector also requires positive training return after removing two best training tokens. Cash is allowed.

The catalog includes closed-equity drawdown scaling, daily drawdown scaling, realized daily loss stops, profit-related size reductions, loss-streak scaling, pauses, spacing and gradual recovery. The closed-trade planner calculates causal sizes first; equivalent plans share the existing candle accounting replay.
