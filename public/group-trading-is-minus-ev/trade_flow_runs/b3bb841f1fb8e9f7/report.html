Daily selection replay

The primary account size is $1,000. Stakes cannot exceed 1% of the preceding 60-second economic volume. Entry and exit each incur a cost proxy equal to the median prior transaction gas plus MEV fees, in addition to the proceeds haircut. At least five earlier swaps are needed for those estimates. These are sensitivity assumptions, not executable-size measurements.

Primary robust_5pct requires positive 5%/10% haircut training, at least 14 trades/10 contracts, <=5% candle drawdown, and positive 10%-cost replay after excluding the two best training tokens. Selection maximizes 10%-cost training return.

What was measured

Fifteen-, sixty- and 180-second pre-call windows: buy/sell volume, net transaction flow, unique recipients, wallet volume concentration, two-sided trading, bursts, price changes, range, VWAP, fee burden, deposits and withdrawals. Filters combine these with fine cap bands and caller categories. Sizing compares flat, concentration, flow and volatility multipliers.

Default features stop five seconds before the call. One- and fifteen-second lags are saved as sensitivity replays. Routes net target-token quantities per transaction and recipient. Fees count once per transaction. No wallet is attributed to a Telegram member. The volume window does not establish historical pool reserves or AMM impact.
